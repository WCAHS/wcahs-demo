Who this clinic is for

WCAHS wants to help families with multiple household cats and/or dogs. This clinic is sponsored for residents of:

Elysian Janesville Waseca Waterville

Outside those towns, or have just one pet? Apply anyway. WCAHS reviews every application individually and makes exceptions — there's a place on the form to tell us about your situation.

What's included

Covered by WCAHS

  • • Spay or neuter surgery
  • • Rabies vaccination
  • • Pre-surgery exam & nail trim
  • • Pain medication and recovery collar

Available at extra cost

  • • Combo vaccine (FVRCP for cats, DAPPV for dogs)
  • • Bordetella / kennel cough (dogs)
  • • Microchip with registration
  • • Flea, tick & mite prevention
  • • Dewormer

These are MNSNAP services paid directly to MNSNAP on the day of surgery. Tell us below what you're interested in and we'll go over costs when we call.

Please note

This clinic is for pets that live in your home. It is not for trapped (TNR), feral or community cats. If you're caring for community cats, contact us and we'll point you toward the right program.

MNSNAP serves Minnesota pet owners who take part in a needs-based assistance program, or who earn under roughly $45,180 as an individual or $93,600 as a household. If you're unsure whether that's you, apply and we'll help you sort it out.
